The five Muslim prayers are Fajr, Dhuhr, Asr, Maghrib, and Isha, and are performed at specific times of the day. The times are based on the position of the sun. 

Prayer times 
•⁠  ⁠Fajr: Before sunrise, at dawn, 2 Rakat
•⁠  ⁠Dhuhr: After the sun passes its highest point, at midday, 4 Rakat
•⁠  ⁠Asr: In the late afternoon, 4 Rakat
•⁠  ⁠Maghrib: At dusk or just after sunset, 3 Rakat
Isha: Between sunset and midnight, 4 Rakat
