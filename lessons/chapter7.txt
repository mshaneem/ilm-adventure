The First 10 Names of Allah and Their Meanings

1. Ar-Rahman
The Most Gracious — the One whose mercy and kindness reaches all of creation.

2. Ar-Raheem
The Most Merciful — the One who shows special, lasting mercy to the believers.

3. Al-Malik
The King — the Sovereign and Owner of everything that exists.

4. Al-Quddus
The Most Holy — the Pure One, free from any fault or imperfection.

5. As-Salam
The Source of Peace — the One who is perfect and gives safety and peace to others.

6. Al-Mu'min
The Giver of Faith — the One who grants security and keeps His promises.

7. Al-Muhaymin
The Guardian — the Protector and Watcher over all of creation.

8. Al-Aziz
The Almighty — the All-Powerful who can never be overcome.

9. Al-Jabbar
The Compeller — the One whose will is always carried out, and who mends what is broken.

10. Al-Mutakabbir
The Supreme — the Greatest, the One who is truly worthy of all majesty and pride.
