🌙🕋 The Amazing Journey of Hajj! 🕋🌙

✨ Day 1 – Making Intention & Wearing Ihram
Pilgrims begin Hajj by making a sincere intention for Allah 🤍
They enter a special state called Ihram and wear simple clothes to show that everyone is equal before Allah.

Pilgrims then:
🕋 Walk around the Kaaba 7 times (Tawaf)
🏃 Walk between the hills of Safa and Marwa (Sai), remembering Hajar (AS) searching for water for baby Ismail.

🏕️ Mina – The Tent City
Next, pilgrims travel to Mina, a city filled with white tents.
They spend the day praying, reading Quran, making dua, and remembering Allah 🤲

🌟 Day 2 – The Special Day of Arafat
Pilgrims travel to Mount Arafat, one of the most important places in Hajj.
This is where Prophet Muhammad ﷺ gave his last sermon.

Pilgrims spend the whole day:
🤲 Making dua
💛 Asking Allah for forgiveness
📿 Worshipping Allah from noon until sunset
Many Muslims around the world also fast on this blessed day.

🌌 Muzdalifah – Sleeping Under the Stars
After sunset, pilgrims travel to Muzdalifah.
They sleep outdoors under the night sky ⭐ and collect 49 small pebbles for the next days.

🪨 Day 3 – Stoning the Devil & Eid al-Adha
Pilgrims return to Mina and throw 7 pebbles at a large pillar. This reminds Muslims how Prophet Ibrahim (AS) refused to listen to Shaytan and obeyed Allah instead.

🐑 Muslims also remember Prophet Ibrahim’s sacrifice on Eid al-Adha by sacrificing an animal and sharing the meat with people in need.

🪨 Days 4 & 5 – Continuing the Rami
Pilgrims continue throwing pebbles at the three pillars each day to show that they reject evil and choose goodness.

✂️ Day 6 – Completing Hajj
Men shave or trim their hair, and women cut a tiny piece of hair to show a fresh beginning.

Pilgrims then return to Makkah to:
🕋 Perform a final Tawaf
🏃 Complete Sai again

This marks the completion of Hajj! 🎉
💛 Hajj teaches Muslims about:
🤝 Equality
🕊️ Patience
💖 Kindness
🙏 Obeying Allah
🤲 Helping others
