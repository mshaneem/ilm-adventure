📖 Today we learnt about Prophet Muhammad's life before prophethood. Read the summary below about the life of Prophet Muhammad ﷺ before Prophethood, then ask someone to test you to see how much you remember!

•⁠  ⁠Prophet Muhammad ﷺ was born in Mecca in 570 AD, around 1400–1500 years ago, into the tribe of Quraysh.

•⁠  ⁠His mother’s name was Amina and his father was Abdullah bin Abdul Muttalib.

•⁠  ⁠He was a descendant of Prophet Ishmael (Ismail) عليه السلام, around 40 generations later.

•⁠  ⁠His father passed away before he was born.

•⁠  ⁠For the first years of his life, he was cared for by a Bedouin wet nurse named Halimah. This was a common custom at the time because the desert environment was considered healthier and better for raising strong children. Mecca also had diseases spreading at the time.

•⁠  ⁠He returned to his mother at around age 2, but Halimah requested to keep him longer, so he stayed with her until around age 5.

•⁠  ⁠Around age 5, he returned to his mother. They travelled to Medina (then called Yathrib) to visit relatives, but on the journey back his mother passed away.

•⁠  ⁠He was then raised by his grandfather Abdul Muttalib, who loved him dearly. After about 2 years, his grandfather also passed away.

•⁠  ⁠He was then cared for by his uncle Abu Talib, who treated him with great care and kindness.

•⁠  ⁠As a young boy, Prophet Muhammad ﷺ worked as a shepherd for his uncle.

•⁠  ⁠As he grew older, he also helped Abu Talib with trading and business journeys.

•⁠  ⁠Prophet Muhammad ﷺ became known among the people for his honesty, purity, fairness, good character and trustworthy behaviour.

•⁠  ⁠Because of this, he was given the title “Al-Ameen” — meaning “The Trustworthy”.

•⁠  ⁠Khadijah رضي الله عنها was a wealthy merchant from Mecca who admired his character and honesty.

•⁠  ⁠She proposed marriage to Prophet Muhammad ﷺ. They married when he was 25 years old and she was 40.

•⁠  ⁠All of this happened before Prophet Muhammad ﷺ received Prophethood at the age of 40.
