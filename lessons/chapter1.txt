The Five Pillars of Islam are the core acts of worship and practice in Islam:

1.⁠ ⁠Shahada (Declaration of Faith)
    * To testify that:
        “There is no god worthy of worship except Allah, and Muhammad is the Messenger of Allah.”
2.⁠ ⁠Salah (Prayer)
    * Performing the five daily prayers at prescribed times.
3.⁠ ⁠Zakat (Almsgiving)
    * Giving a portion of one’s wealth to those in need, typically 2.5% of eligible savings each year.
4.⁠ ⁠Sawm (Fasting during Ramadan)
    * Abstaining from food, drink, and other physical needs from dawn to sunset during the month of Ramadan.
5.⁠ ⁠Hajj (Pilgrimage to Mecca)
    * Making the pilgrimage to Mecca at least once in a lifetime if physically and financially able.

These pillars provide the basic framework for a Muslim’s faith, worship, and daily life.

Shahada:

Ashhadu an lā ilāha illā Allāh, wa ashhadu anna Muḥammadan rasūlu Allāh.

“I bear witness that there is no god worthy of worship except Allah, and I bear witness that Muhammad is the Messenger of Allah.”
