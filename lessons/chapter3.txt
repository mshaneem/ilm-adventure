Salaams Kids, 

Please complete the following for homework:

Memorise the following duas said during Salat and their meaning. Practice your Salat and saying these duas during prayer. Send what you have memorised by voice message. You can have a bit more time next week to complete if needed.

During Ruku (Bowing):
Transliteration: "Subhaana Rabbiyal 'Azeem" (3 times).
Translation: "Glory to my Lord the Exalted."

Rising from Ruku:
Transliteration:"Sami'Allaahu liman hamidah" (Allah hears whoever praises Him).
Transliteration: "Rabbanaa wa lakal hamd" (Our Lord, and to You is praise).

During Sujud (Prostration):
Transliteration: "Subhaana Rabbiyal 'A'la" (3 times).
Translation: "Glory to my Lord the Most High."

Between the Two Prostrations:
Transliteration: "Rabbighfir lee, Rabbighfir lee".
Translation: "Lord forgive me, Lord forgive me."
